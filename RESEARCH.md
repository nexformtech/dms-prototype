## 4. Programme structure

Instagram bio + admissions copy give the ladder: **Pre-Nursery → Nursery → KG (LKG/UKG) → PREP →
Grades 1 & 2**. This matches India's NEP 2020 *Foundational Stage* (ages 3–8: three years of
pre-primary + Grades 1–2) and the NIPUN Bharat focus on Foundational Literacy & Numeracy — the
prototype's curriculum language (phonics, early reading, early maths, play-way/"learn by doing")
is framed on that basis. The school's sister-site philosophy phrase "Learn by Doing" also appears
in Deeksha branding elsewhere and informed the pedagogy copy.

- **BeTheme "Kindergarten 4" (muffingroup)** & **Dribbble kindergarten tag** — crayon/pastel palette,
  rounded sticker cards, wavy section dividers, illustrated mascots, big playful display type.
These patterns were adapted: stats band, programme cards, testimonial slider, FAQ accordion,
celebration calendar, wavy dividers, sticker-card UI.
