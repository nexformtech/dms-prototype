Since 1999

The little school with a big heart

Deeksha Model School was set up way back in 1999 with a mission to impart a quality environment, facilities and education to pre-schoolers — and that mission still guides every single day of ours.

Our story

From one warm classroom to Jaipur's foundational learning centre

What began in 1999 as a small, loving pre-school is today recognised as a leading centre for early-years development and foundational education. Deeksha Model School is a registered and recognised institution that meets the highest standards of teaching young minds.

With a team of professionally qualified and experienced childcare professionals, we deliver outstanding care and education through the most precious years — the foundational stage, from age 2 to 9.

We stay exclusive and intentional: only foundational classes, so every decision, wall, rhyme and routine is designed for little learners.

  • ✓ Exclusive foundational school — Playgroup to Grade 3
  • ✓ Registered & recognised institution
  • ✓ NEP-aligned foundational stage (play-way + foundational literacy & numeracy)
