Parent FAQs

Questions? We've got answers

Admissions for the 2026–27 session are open now and continue through the year, subject to seat availability. Because batches are intentionally small, Playgroup and Nursery seats usually fill first — we recommend enquiring by January–February.

Deeksha is an English-medium school. In the earliest years, teachers comfortably blend Hindi and English so every child feels secure, moving to full English fluency as they grow — phonics and language play do the heavy lifting.

Classes run Monday to Saturday in the morning (indicatively 7:45 AM – 1:30 PM, with shorter sessions for Playgroup). Exact batch timings for each program are shared at admission and on the class notice board.

We are a neighbourhood foundational school with an accessible location in Hanuman Nagar Extension / Jaswant Nagar — most families live within a short drop. We currently encourage parent drop-and-pick, which also gives you a daily window with your child's teacher.

Child-sized, rounded furniture; guarded stairs and windows; secure entry with verified pick-up; trained staff on every floor; hygiene and sanitisation routines; and first-aid trained teachers on campus at all times. Safety is our first curriculum.

Our team comprises professionally qualified and experienced childcare professionals — trained early-childhood educators (NTT/TTT/B.Ed. backgrounds) who receive ongoing in-house training on play-way pedagogy, child psychology and safety.

Children bring a small healthy tiffin from home; we schedule a supervised fruit/snack break where teachers encourage good manners, sharing and hygiene. Special “cooking-without-fire” days happen in class as an activity.

Completely normal, and completely handled. Our phased orientation (parent stays → short stays → full session) plus a loving didi in every class eases separation anxiety, usually within one to two weeks. We keep you posted with updates until the tears turn into waves-hello.
