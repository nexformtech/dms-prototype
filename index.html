    Why Deeksha

    A second home, not just a school

    Set up way back in 1999, Deeksha Model School began with one mission — to give pre-schoolers a quality environment, facilities and education. Today we are recognised as a leading centre for early-years development and foundational education in Jaipur.

    • ✓ Warm, safe and caring — the same security children feel at home
    • ✓ Professionally qualified, experienced childcare professionals
    • ✓ English medium with an affordable fee structure
    • ✓ Strong school–parent partnership with open, regular communication
    • ✓ Accessible location in Hanuman Nagar Extension, Jaipur
