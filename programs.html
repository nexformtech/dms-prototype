Six programs · one foundation

Every age gets its own kind of magic

Our foundational journey follows how children grow — from the first sensory discoveries of Playgroup to the confident reading, writing and calculating of Grade 3. Same philosophy throughout: learn by doing, grow by playing.

🧸 Playgroup

Ages 2 – 3 · morning batch
  • Sensory play, sand & water exploration
  • Action rhymes, peek-a-boo language games
  • Self-help basics: tidy-up, wash-up, share-up
  • Plenty of lap-time, cuddles and calm routines

    • 🎨 Nursery

    Ages 3 – 4 · morning batch
  • Storytime, puppet play & picture talk
  • Crayons, finger painting, clay & craft
  • First phonics sounds through songs
  • Outdoor play, balancing & ball games

    • 🔤 LKG

    Ages 4 – 5 · morning batch
  • Phonics & letter-sound games (pre-reading)
  • Pre-writing: strokes, patterns, pencil grip
  • Numbers 1–20 through counting play
  • Show-and-tell, rhymes & library time

    • 📚 UKG

    Ages 5 – 6 · morning batch
  • Blending, sight words & first reading books
  • Writing words & simple sentences
  • Addition–subtraction with concrete objects
  • EVS: my family, my Jaipur, my world

    • 🚀 PREP

    Ages 5.5 – 6.5 · morning batch
  • Fluent reading & neat, confident writing
  • Mental maths, patterns & problem play
  • Concentration, routine & study skills
  • Public speaking: little presentations

    • 🏆 Grades 1 – 3

    Ages 6 – 9 · morning batch
  • Language arts: reading, writing, grammar play
  • Maths: operations, shapes, measurement
  • EVS / science & social discovery projects
  • Art, music, yoga, sports & library periods

    • Age criteria as on 31 March of the admission year (indicative). Exact cut-offs & batch timings are shared at admission — see admissions.

    How we teach

    Learn by doing, grow by playing

    Research is clear: high-quality early education changes life outcomes. So our classrooms are workshops, not lecture halls — children touch, build, sing, act, count and question their way to understanding.

    Every week balances these learning areas, mapped to the NEP 2020 foundational stage and NIPUN Bharat goals:

    🔤 Phonics & early reading 🔢 Early maths & logic 🌍 EVS & nature study 🎨 Art & craft 🎵 Music & dance ⚽ Outdoor play & sports 🧘 Yoga & wellbeing 🤝 Social & emotional skills 🪔 Values & culture 💻 Smart-class activities
    A day at Deeksha

    Our joyful daily rhythm

    Predictable routines make little children feel safe — inside that rhythm, every hour hides a new adventure. (Timings indicative; exact schedule shared by class.)

    1. 7:45 – 8:15Welcome & free play 🎈

      Soft landing at school — hugs, hello-song, and choosing a corner to explore.

    2. 8:15 – 8:40Morning circle & yoga 🧘

      Prayer, news-time, tiny asanas and breathing games to wake up bodies and minds.

    3. 8:40 – 9:30Language & phonics play 🔤

      Sounds, stories, rhymes and letter games — the roots of reading.

    4. 9:30 – 9:50Fruit break 🍎

      Healthy tummy, good manners — we wash hands, share and say thank you.

    5. 9:50 – 10:30Number games & early maths 🔢

      Counting, sorting, patterns and shape hunts with real objects.

    6. 10:30 – 11:10Outdoor play ⚽

      Slide, swings, ball games and races — gross motor skills and big laughs.

    7. 11:10 – 12:00Activity rotation 🎨

      Art & craft, music & dance, nature corner or smart-class — on a happy rota.

    8. 12:00 – 12:30Story time & goodbye circle 📖

      One last story, one last song, stars on hands — and a proud walk home.
